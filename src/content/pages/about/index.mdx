Welcome — I'm **Kenneth Henseler**, a systems and platform engineer by day and an open-source builder, researcher, and podcaster after hours.

**Musings of My Today** is my independent digital notebook, built as content-as-code with Astro, MDX, and Cloudflare Workers (keeping every original WordPress permalink intact). It is where engineering post-mortems, ancient history, astronomy, sequenced playlists, and short fiction live in one place without an algorithm sorting what gets seen.

## What's here

- **[Engineering](/section/engineering/)** — Platform architecture, AI coding agent harnesses that misbehave, and the World of Warcraft addons I build live on stream.
- **[History & Politics](/section/history-politics/)** — Deep dives into institutional systems, logistics, and the collisions between modern infrastructure and older history.
- **[Science & Nature](/section/science-nature/)** — Astronomy observations, biology, origins research, and notes from the trail.
- **[Signal](/section/signal/)** — Harmonic, Camelot-sequenced monthly playlists and lyrical deconstructions.
- **[Field Notes](/section/field-notes/)** — Short fiction, poetry, films, cigars, food, travel, and personal experiments.

## Podcasts & live broadcasts

- **[Podcasts](/podcasts/)** — I produce two companion shows with full transcripts and chaptered RSS feeds: **Runtime Reality** (technical & infrastructure post-mortems) and **The Chronos Archive** (where ancient history collides with modern systems).
