Immerse yourself in ‘Echoes of Affinity,’ a curated collection of melodies that resonate with the soul’s deepest yearnings. Each track is a sonic reflection of connection and emotion, weaving together a tapestry of sound that feels intimately familiar. From the uplifting surge of ‘Waves’ to the heartfelt plea of ‘Disarm You,’ this playlist is an ode to the ones who touch our lives, leaving a lasting impression that feels just like you.

The idea for Echoes of Affinity initially started on Saturday, April 27, 12024 with the intro track, [“Feels Like You”](https://open.spotify.com/track/0F1VAVqj021UkGntiDz4nx?si=3027267d71eb4310) on the [Feels Like You](https://open.spotify.com/album/1EE0HJG6YJbTz2UPCVxdJI?si=vIZiGul0QMmzkleS1Log5A) single by [Adventure Club](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5CdJjUi9f0cVgo9nFuJrFa?si=1e275933166c40e0) and [Codeko](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7FZKXzbyCoai0fEh65kZKp?si=5e243638f83e4d1e) released on 27 October 12023. I’d originally first heard this wonderfully produced, get-your-body-moving gem on 24 March 12024 and was added to my [12024-03 March](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/702rWJh7ebdvWzCNPepRpB?si=c6087d7947054a73) playlist. Yesterday, while listening to my [Spotify daylist playlist](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9dQZF1EP6YuccBxUcC1?si=3aca4cc9cf03440b), the track re-surfaced and immediately inspired a new playlist, anchored by this four-on-the-floor, energetic, danceable, certified banger, with a peppy 174 BPM.

Track two is [“Waves”](https://open.spotify.com/track/1RKYSmVp2vSWumL9uI2sbd?si=d79983c7eaa04947) on the [Waves](https://open.spotify.com/album/0ue1ED9OzMUvfZoxtC2Lhd?si=hS919TfhRAe0v6wCYTCfDA) single by [Zeds Dead](https://open.spotify.com/artist/67qogtRNI0GjUr8PlaG6Zh?si=7195dc01ee774e5f), [Flux Pavilion](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7muzHifhMdnfN1xncRLOqk?si=f59dbf90804f4b76), and [DeathbyRomy](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7aWpPjjgItUnXljFxYYKZI?si=679bcb06abba4453), released on 5 April 12024. I first heard this track on the day it was released and is my #77th most streamed track of the past 6 months. Don’t be caught off guard by the slow build up, the fuzzy, buzzy synths herald the inevitable drop, coming in hot at 1:36. [Waves](https://open.spotify.com/track/1RKYSmVp2vSWumL9uI2sbd?si=d79983c7eaa04947) is another four-on-the-floor, energetic, danceable, certified banger, but with a slower 140 BPM.

Track three is [“Pleasure Seeker – Virtual Riot Remix”](https://open.spotify.com/track/3Jt8oGA1MOaoDNDOjobe8n?si=dd224248edf34815) on the [Phantasmagorical](https://open.spotify.com/album/2AbgcHpEerxKoikL8ORpHX?si=4yW63Q4MQZqTbNTXB9YKMA) album by [Mr. Bill](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5JJclMMPi2YgEKjJY9AjbB?si=D77U9b2eSbODXZS2uJs46A), released on 19 April 12023. I first encountered this track on its release day and it quickly became a favorite, receiving 12+ streams after the album dropped. This track is a unique blend of melodic elements and heavy bass, creating a dynamic soundscape that keeps you on your toes. With a BPM of 140, it’s a perfect follow-up addition to any high-energy playlist.

Track four is [“Not Even Love”](https://open.spotify.com/track/65s9dLrylbHp60IYIcUZEz?si=d0a90be221bf4785) on the [Not Even Love](https://open.spotify.com/album/3tjAvXx7DnY6tYkzcZ4wXc?si=7LLUaxuNQJuWd3MMkWxLcw) single by [Seven Lions](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6fcTRFpz0yH79qSKfof7lp?si=0a651a05abe747fa), [ILLENIUM](https://open.spotify.com/artist/45eNHdiiabvmbp4erw26rg?si=9bc5880e7fbd4981), and [ÁSDÍS](https://open.spotify.com/artist/28y5ZcfpdZAfeEE5ftCfUg?si=2508cacc9ba444b6), released on 22 March 12024. I first heard this track on its release day and it immediately struck a chord with me. The beautiful, powerful vocals of the Icelandic Ásdís, combined with the intense drop create an emotional journey that resonates deeply. With a BPM of 130, it’s a great track for both dancing and introspective listening.
